# MoA Cam — Description & Purpose

**MoA Cam** is a project by **Masters Of All (MoA)** built around a simple idea: **the tools available to create should not be the reason an idea never gets created.**

Modern smartphones already contain capable camera sensors, lenses, processors, microphones, and displays. Yet much of that capability is hidden behind simplified camera applications, limited controls, proprietary software, or expensive professional equipment. At the same time, creators who want more control often have to choose between expensive cameras, complicated setups, paid applications, or solutions that only solve one part of the problem.

**MoA Cam aims to bridge that gap.**

It transforms a compatible smartphone into a more capable **cinema camera, production tool, and professional webcam**, giving the user greater control over the hardware they already own.

### What problem does it solve?

MoA Cam addresses several problems:

* **Limited camera control:** Smartphone camera apps often hide or restrict professional controls such as ISO, shutter, focus, and white balance.
* **Expensive equipment:** Professional camera systems and accessories can be inaccessible to students, beginners, and independent creators.
* **Unused hardware:** A capable phone may already contain the sensor and hardware needed for many creative applications, but users may not have the software to fully utilize it.
* **Separate tools for separate jobs:** Users often need one application for recording, another for webcam use, another for monitoring, and additional software for processing.
* **The gap between mobile and professional workflows:** Smartphones are convenient and portable, while professional cameras provide greater control. MoA Cam attempts to bring aspects of both approaches together.
* **Accessibility:** Advanced camera workflows should not be limited to people who can afford expensive equipment or proprietary solutions.

### What does MoA Cam do?

MoA Cam provides a unified workflow in which the smartphone can be used as:

**A standalone camera** — for recording directly on the phone with greater control over the image.

**A studio camera** — where the phone acts as the camera and a computer can handle additional processing, monitoring, recording, and production tasks.

**A webcam** — allowing the phone's camera to be used as a camera source for computers, streaming, meetings, recording software, and other applications.

Depending on the device's hardware capabilities, MoA Cam is designed to provide controls such as **ISO, shutter speed/shutter angle, exposure, focus, white balance, frame rate, lens selection, resolution, zoom, audio input, monitoring, recording, and image-processing tools**.

### Philosophy

MoA Cam follows the broader philosophy of **Masters Of All**:

> **If the tools already exist, why shouldn't people be able to use them to their full potential?**

Rather than treating a smartphone as *just a phone*, MoA Cam explores what can be achieved when its existing hardware is combined with purpose-built software.

The goal is not simply to make another camera application.

**The goal is to unlock capability, reduce barriers, and turn hardware that people already have into a tool for creating, exploring, and bringing ideas to life.**
